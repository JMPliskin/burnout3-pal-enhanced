Burnout 3 PAL Enhanced 0.2.0-alpha - Windows x64
LOCAL RELEASE CANDIDATE - PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION PENDING RIGHTS REVIEW

Extract the complete package and start Burnout3-PAL-Enhanced.exe.
Choose your own European SLES_525.84 ISO. No ISO, BIOS, extracted assets or saves
are supplied. No Python, compiler or BAT is needed to play an approved package.
Requires Windows 10 1903+ x64, AVX2 and a Vulkan 1.3-capable GPU/current driver.

F9 or controller L3+R3: PC Settings. F1: FPS Counter. F12: full GS capture.
AA OFF/FXAA/SMAA changes live. GPU Thread changes require restarting the game.
CLI overrides take precedence and are labelled in F9. A full capture can pause
execution. Original PAL language/50/60 Hz screens remain; Spanish is tested.
New profiles default to 2x, 16:9, pacing ON, VSync OFF, AA OFF and fullscreen.
Explicit saved preferences, including windowed mode, are respected.

Settings and ISO: %LOCALAPPDATA%/Burnout3-PAL-Enhanced/pc_settings.ini
Profiles/cards:  %LOCALAPPDATA%/Burnout3-PAL-Enhanced/memcard/
Logs:            %LOCALAPPDATA%/Burnout3-PAL-Enhanced/logs/
Upgrade into a separate folder without deleting your personal data. Back up
memcard first. Do not run two instances against the same writable card.
The player asks again if its saved ISO is unavailable; saves are not erased.

New: regional presentation, texture page index, GS16 SIMD, live AA and F9 options,
separate menu/capture controls, bounded audio recovery and fullscreen defaults.
Audio recovery covers tested capture/window movement, not every interruption.
Minor 3x skybox seam remains; heavy 4x scenes may slow down. VU Fast, SSAA in F9,
120 FPS, custom music and LAN are not validated PAL features.
See RELEASE_NOTES_v0.2.0-alpha.md. No guaranteed frame rate is claimed.
